Exercise Library

Quick cues for the movements that matter most in this block.

Power clean

Keep the bar close, extend violently, then receive it with fast elbows. Use light loads until technique feels automatic.

Hang power clean

Start from the hang, load the hips, extend hard, and catch above a full squat.

Front squat

Elbows high, trunk braced, knees track naturally over toes, stay balanced through the whole foot.

Trap-bar deadlift

Brace, push the floor away, stand tall. For this program, rep speed matters more than maximal load.

Loaded tibialis raise

Seated is fine. Pull the forefoot toward the shin through full range, pause at the top, lower slowly.

Poliquin step-up

Use a small elevation. Let the knee travel forward under control and keep the working foot planted.

Spanish squat

Band behind the knees, sit back while keeping the torso tall. Useful as a warm-up/isometric when comfortable.

Lateral bound

Jump sideways and own the landing. Knee tracks over the foot; don't rush the next rep.

Accelerations

Build speed aggressively over the first 10–30 yards. Full recovery between reps. Quality beats fatigue.

Train for strength, power, conditioning, and longevity.
