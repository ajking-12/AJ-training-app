Rules for the block

  • Power work comes first. Stop a power exercise when speed or landing quality drops.
  • Main strength work starts around RPE 6–7 and builds toward 7–8.
  • Easy second sessions stay easy. They are activity/recovery, not another hard workout.
  • For pain beyond normal training discomfort—especially knee pain that changes your mechanics—reduce or stop the provoking movement.
  • Nutrition drives the cut. Training's job is to preserve/build strength and athleticism.
Train for strength, power, conditioning, and longevity.
